The perspective

Credibility that travels.

I am Rizwan Bukhari, a senior journalist with over 24 years of experience working with various organizations across the world. Throughout my career, I have covered diverse stories, aiming to inform, engage, and connect with audiences through credible and impactful journalism.

Alongside my professional work, I run a YouTube channel, FB RIZ TV, where I create high-quality, professionally produced content. My channel features a blend of entertainment, informative segments, exploration, and travel vlogs from around the world, reflecting my passion for storytelling beyond traditional media.
